How the buy-box score is calculated

A parcel starts at zero and earns points in the parts below, up to each part's maximum. The score ranks land against ACMI's defense-industrial buy-box (50 to 500+ net usable acres, industrial or ag land with a path to industrial use, high-voltage power, truck or rail access, a defense driver, and no floodway, material wetlands or contamination).
