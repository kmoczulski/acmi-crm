How the dates and dollars are worked out
  • Everything counts from the PSA signing date: the round's date plus the days it allows to get a PSA signed (or a date you set).
  • Final zoning approval and the outside date are the end of the month that many months after PSA signing. Closing is the days after final zoning approval (or after feasibility, for a phase that doesn't wait on zoning).
  • A deposit for an extension is paid on the day the extension has to be exercised, the end of the period it extends. Extensions "used" are the ones this schedule assumes are exercised.
  • "Walk away here" is cumulative: what ACMI would lose (the non-refundable deposits so far) and what it would get back. Deposits are credited to the price, so cash to close is the price less every deposit.
  • Phase II and later start the number of days you set after the previous phase closes, and run their own feasibility, deposits and closing.
