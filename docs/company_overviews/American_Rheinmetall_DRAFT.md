**Summary.** American Rheinmetall is the U.S. defense business of Rheinmetall AG (Düsseldorf; DAX-listed). It makes combat
vehicles and vehicle components (tracks, drivelines, armor, hulls), medium-caliber ammunition and fuzes. It is one of two
teams building XM30 prototypes, the Army's Bradley replacement. Its munitions arm is assessing sites for new U.S. munitions and
propellant capacity. This matches the two 150–300 acre energetics projects its VP of Business Development described to us
in March 2026.
